# Why wall-bouncing

Establish the issue's Why before drafting the body. Ask one question per message, and attach the answer you expect to the recommended option as a hypothesis. Explore with Read or ugrep before asking anything the codebase can settle. Stop asking and move to drafting once the three points below are readable from the description, or once you can predict the answer to the question you would ask next.

| Question                                      | Where it lands in the body |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Who needs this                                | What & Why                 |
| What pain exists and what the evidence is     | What & Why                 |
| What counts as success as a measurable result | Acceptance Criteria        |
