# Why wall-bouncing

Settle the issue's Why before drafting the body. Ask one question per message. Attach the answer you expect to the recommended option as a hypothesis. Anything the codebase can settle goes to Read or ugrep before it becomes a question. Once the three points below are readable from the description, stop asking and move to drafting. Do the same once you can predict the answer to the question you would ask next.

| Question                                      | Where it lands in the body |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Who needs this                                | What & Why                 |
| What pain exists, and what the evidence is    | What & Why                 |
| What counts as success as a measurable result | Acceptance Criteria        |
