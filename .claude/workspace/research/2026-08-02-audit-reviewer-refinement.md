## Purpose

Establish, for the 18 agents under `agents/reviewers/`, which lenses are unreachable or absorbed elsewhere; which structural properties of `workflows/audit.js` degrade finding quality at the Challenge and Verify stages; and how the measured wall time divides between the parallel Review stage and the serial Challenge / Verify / Integrate / Snapshot tail.

## Constraints

| Category    | Constraint                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| OUTCOME     | Quality gates must not be bypassable at LLM discretion (Behavior 1). A stage whose pruning rule is undetermined between two contradictory instructions, and whose verdicts never reach a durable record, is discretion rather than a gate                          |
| OUTCOME     | Verification is delegated to the deterministic hook and workflow layer, and the human reviews only the judgment residue (Behavior 2). A tail that discards root causes into a flat 4-field list pushes judgment back to the human                                  |
| Mirror      | Every reviewer or workflow edit lands in `.ja/` first and mirrors in the same commit; tests live on the English side only                                                                                                                                          |
| Runtime     | `parallel()` is defined by the workflow runtime, not in this repository. The 6-agent ceiling and the failure-isolation contract are observed and corroborated by ADR-0081:67, not documented. The same holds for how `model` and `effort` at the call site resolve |
| Inherited   | ADR-0058 is accepted but deferred; `use-context-*` skill directories remain, and four of them bind reviewer agents. Retiring a bound reviewer breaks its skill                                                                                                     |
| Measurement | Reviewer findings drift substantially between runs, so a before and after count on a single change proves nothing. Every candidate above is argued from routing reachability, definition text, or transcript evidence, with counts used only for volume ranking    |

## Disconfirmation Check
